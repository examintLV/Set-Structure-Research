# Analysis Playbook

Use the practical matchup workflow in [`PLAYER_ANALYSIS_GUIDE.md`](../../PLAYER_ANALYSIS_GUIDE.md). It replaces the former numbered-filter research sequence.

In brief: confirm format and surface; gather comparable, sourced player results and relevant serve/return evidence; compare both players and opponent context; assess the possible scorelines from the named player's perspective; and report the leading scenario, supporting evidence, sample sizes, caveats, and uncertainty. Keep best-of-three and best-of-five matches separate. Do not turn raw historical frequencies into precise forecasts or invent probabilities.
